# v0.1 Boundary Policy — Non-Goals

> Scope: the v0.1 Monthly Close Harness + evidence-driven accounting
> foundations (slices S1–S6). Status: binding policy for v0.1.

This document is the authoritative statement of what v0.1 explicitly does NOT
do. Every non-goal below maps to at least one operating policy in this
directory (REQ-SKPT-005; SC-SKPT-002).

## Non-goal 1 — No autonomous filing with the Peruvian tax authority

**v0.1 never files anything autonomously with the Peruvian tax authority.**
The harness prepares evidence-cited proposals; filing and submission belong to
the responsible professional. See [authority-policy.md](authority-policy.md).

## Non-goal 2 — No irreversible posting without approval

**v0.1 never performs an irreversible posting without explicit approval.**
Every irreversible action requires a complete scope binding, a cited
candidate, an R2 approval, and a signed receipt. See
[authority-policy.md](authority-policy.md).

## Non-goal 3 — No free interpretation without evidence

**v0.1 never accepts a free or uncited interpretation without evidence.**
Every conclusion cites evidence-graph node ids; uncited conclusions are
rejected. See [evidence-policy.md](evidence-policy.md).

## Non-goal 4 — No material tax decision made by an LLM alone

**v0.1 never makes a material tax decision by an LLM alone.** Model output is
untrusted proposal data until deterministic checks, evidence binding, and
professional approval succeed. See [evidence-policy.md](evidence-policy.md).

## Non-goal 5 — No replacement of the responsible professional

**v0.1 never replaces the responsible professional.** Every protected action
ends in a human decision: approval, correction, or filing. The harness
prepares and evidences; the professional decides.

## Non-goal 6 — No silent modification of closed periods

**v0.1 never silently modifies a closed period.** Corrections are new
`correction` missions with their own scope, evidence, approval, and receipts.
See [closed-period-policy.md](closed-period-policy.md).

## Post-v0.1 roadmap behavior is absent and explicitly denied

The post-v0.1 roadmap (SIRE integration, advanced reconciliation, AP/AR,
monthly taxes, continuous audit) is **out of scope and explicitly denied in
v0.1**: none of that behavior is provided, and no fallback path exposes it.
A request that needs roadmap behavior fails closed with a clear "not provided
in v0.1" denial rather than degrading into an unapproved approximation.
