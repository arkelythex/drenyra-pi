Guide a new user through the FSD lifecycle using their real codebase.

## Process

1. **Explain FSD:** The 6 fiscal phases and R0-R3 risk tiers.
2. **Detect project:** Check for fiscal packages, skills, existing periods.
3. **Run /drenyra:preflight** to verify setup.
4. **Run /fsd:init** to start a sample period.
5. **Show tools:** verify_fiscal_phase, record_receipt, run_fiscal_lens.
6. **Show guards:** Money type, RUC scope, destructive ops.
7. **First task:** Walk through propose → spec → design → apply.
