Close and archive a completed FSD lifecycle.

## Process

1. **Verify** all tasks are complete and tests pass.
2. **Verify** all RED receipts are recorded.
3. **Verify** all gates passed (R0-R3 approvals).
4. **Write summary:** What changed, what was the impact, what gates passed.
5. **Archive** the FSD state and mark period phase as complete.
