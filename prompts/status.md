# /drenyra:status

Show the verification status of the pinned Drenyra AI runtime plus the active
scope and mission projection for the current company and fiscal period.

## Output

- Runtime verdict (verified / pending-release / failed) against the pin.
- Active company RUC and fiscal period; scope completeness and missing
  elements when incomplete.
- Active mission: status, next authorized action, and wait reason when
  waiting for evidence, approval, or a policy gate.
- Structured JSON plus a concise human summary.
