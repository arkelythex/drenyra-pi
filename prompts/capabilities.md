# /drenyra:capabilities

    Report engine capabilities plus harness capabilities: the four authority
    modes, the registered command surface, and the ten canonical scope elements.

    ## Output

    - Engine capabilities from the Drenyra AI runtime, mirroring the Design 03
      `drenyra-ai capabilities` surface: available contracts, skills (by layer
      and validity period), jurisdictions, and adapters.
    - Harness capabilities: authority modes, commands, scope elements.
    - Structured JSON plus a concise human summary.
